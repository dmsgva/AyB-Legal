Privacy Policy

AyB Video Publisher · Last updated: November 14, 2026

AyB Video Publisher is used only by the owner of the "Armas y Poder" and "Bikenemy" channels to publish our own videos to our own TikTok accounts.

Data we access

The basic profile of the authorized account (display name and avatar) and the access tokens issued by TikTok.

How we use it

Only to identify the account and publish the videos we create. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are never shared or sold.

Other users

We do not collect, store or process data from any other TikTok user.

Revoking access

The account owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok > Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions.
